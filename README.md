# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

**Excel | Data Analysis | PivotTables | Dashboarding**

An end-to-end e-commerce sales analysis project built in Microsoft Excel. The workbook analyzes 5,000 order records to explore sales and profitability trends, product and category performance, customer behavior, regional performance, payment methods, and discount patterns.

---

## Project Overview

The goal of this project is to turn e-commerce transaction data into useful business insights through Excel-based analysis and visualization.

The central business question explored in the workbook is:

> **Which products and customer segments drive the business?**

The analysis also investigates how performance changes over time and how sales and profit vary across products, categories, regions, age groups, payment methods, and discount bands.

## Business Objectives

- Track sales performance over time.
- Identify categories and products contributing the most sales and profit.
- Examine customer-level sales and identify high-value customers.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions.
- Understand purchasing patterns across customer age groups.
- Review the sales contribution of different payment methods.
- Compare sales across discount bands.

## Dataset

The workbook contains a data sheet named `Ecommerce_Sales_Data_2024_2025` with **5,000 order records** and **20 columns**.

The recorded order dates range from **October 4, 2023 to October 3, 2025**.

### Data fields

| Area | Fields |
|---|---|
| Order information | Order ID, Order Date, Order Month, Order Year |
| Customer information | Age, Age Group, Customer Name |
| Geography | Region, City |
| Product information | Category, Sub-Category, Product Name |
| Transaction details | Quantity, Unit Price, Discount in %, Sales, Profit |
| Derived / classification fields | Profit Margin, Discount Band |
| Payment information | Payment Mode |

## Analysis Questions

The workbook's PivotTable analysis and dashboard address questions such as:

1. How is business performance changing over time?
2. Which product categories contribute to sales and profit?
3. Which products generate the highest sales?
4. Which payment methods are used most, based on sales?
5. Which customers have the highest sales?
6. How do sales vary across customer age groups and regions?
7. How do sales compare across discount bands?
8. How do sales vary by region and category?

## Dashboard

The `DashBoard` sheet contains eight visualizations:

1. **Month-wise Total Sales** — tracks sales across months.
2. **Product Category-wise Sales and Profit** — compares category performance.
3. **Most Used Payment Methods** — compares sales by payment mode.
4. **Top 10 Customers with Highest Sales** — highlights high-sales customers.
5. **Top 10 Highest Sales Products** — ranks products by sales.
6. **Age Group-wise Top Sales Regions** — compares regional sales across age groups.
7. **Discount-wise Sales** — compares sales across discount bands.
8. **Region** — presents the regional analysis included in the workbook.

## Selected Findings

The following findings are calculated from the workbook's transaction data:

- **Home Decor** has the highest total sales among the listed product categories.
- **Furniture** has the highest total profit among the listed product categories.
- **Headphones Accusantium** is the highest-sales product in the dataset.
- **Net Banking** has the highest sales total among the payment methods.
- The **North** region has the highest total profit among the four regions.
- The **High Discount** band contains 3,024 orders, compared with 1,976 orders in the Low Discount band.

These findings describe the workbook data and should not be interpreted as proof that discounts cause higher or lower sales or profit.
