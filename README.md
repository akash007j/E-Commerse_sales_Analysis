E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard
Excel | Data Analysis | Business Intelligence
Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to identify business performance trends, top-performing product categories, customer purchasing patterns, regional sales performance, and the impact of discounts on sales.

The project uses Microsoft Excel to perform data analysis, create PivotTables, calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), and develop an interactive dashboard to generate meaningful business insights.
Business Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze sales performance across different regions.
- Identify the most frequently used payment methods.
- Understand the relationship between discounts and sales performance.
- Identify customer age groups contributing to business profitability.
Dataset Description
The project contains 5,000 e-commerce order records with 20 columns.

Key columns include:
Category	Columns
Order Details	Order ID, Order Date, Order Month, Order Year
Customer Details	Customer Name, Age, Age Group
Location	Region, City
Product Details	Category, Sub-Category, Product Name
Sales Details	Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Sales, Profit
Other Details	Payment Mode, Profit Margin, Discount Band

Key Business Insights
- Total sales generated across the dataset are approximately ₹533.67 million.
- Home Decor is the highest-performing category by sales.
- Furniture and Clothing are also among the top-performing categories.
- Headphones Accusantium is the highest-selling product among the top 10 products.
- Net Banking is the payment method associated with the highest sales.
- The East and North regions show strong profitability contributions from age groups 3 and 4.
- High Discount orders have a higher average discount and account for more orders than Low Discount orders.
